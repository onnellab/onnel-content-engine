Quivra Privacy Policy

한국어 · ONNELLAB

This Privacy Policy applies to the Quivra app provided by ONNELLAB.

Last updated: 2026-07-30

1. Accounts and direct identifiers

Quivra does not require registration or sign-in with a name, email address, or phone number. Except for an optional server feature described below, app content and usage history are not transmitted to ONNELLAB.

2. Data accessed or stored on the device

  • Media files that the user explicitly selects
  • Converted output files and app preferences stored on the device
  • Selected files are converted on the device.
  • Source and converted files are not uploaded to an ONNELLAB server.

3. Server transmission

The app features described above do not transmit file contents or usage history to an ONNELLAB server.

4. Payment information

If an app purchase occurs, the applicable app store processes the payment; ONNELLAB does not directly process card or bank-account details.

5. Advertising, analytics, and sharing

Quivra does not use advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking tools. Data is not sold or shared outside the purposes described above. If the user deliberately hands content to another app or service through the operating-system share features, that service's policy applies.

6. Retention and deletion

App-managed local data can be removed through the applicable delete controls or by uninstalling the app. Source and output files controlled by the user remain under the user's control. ONNELLAB retains no app content on its servers.

7. Security and children's privacy

ONNELLAB applies reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to data that must be transmitted. Quivra is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes and contact

If app functionality or legal or store requirements change, this document will be updated and its last-updated date will change.

Privacy questions or deletion requests: onnellab.app@gmail.com
