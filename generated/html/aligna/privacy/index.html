Aligna Privacy Policy

한국어 · ONNELLAB

This Privacy Policy applies to the Aligna app provided by ONNELLAB.

Last updated: 2026-07-30

1. Accounts and direct identifiers

Aligna does not require registration or sign-in with a name, email address, or phone number. Except for an optional server feature described below, app content and usage history are not transmitted to ONNELLAB.

2. Data accessed or stored on the device

  • Files and file metadata that the user explicitly selects
  • Rename rules, previews, app preferences, and purchase entitlement stored on the device
  • Preview and rename operations occur on the device.
  • Selected files are not uploaded to an ONNELLAB server.
  • On iOS, a newly named copy may be saved instead of changing the original file directly.

3. Server transmission

The app features described above do not transmit file contents or usage history to an ONNELLAB server.

4. Payment information

In-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Neither Aligna nor ONNELLAB accesses or stores card or bank-account details. Product, transaction, and entitlement information supplied by the store may be used to verify and restore purchases.

5. Advertising, analytics, and sharing

Aligna does not use advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking tools. Data is not sold or shared outside the purposes described above. If the user deliberately hands content to another app or service through the operating-system share features, that service's policy applies.

6. Retention and deletion

App-managed local data can be removed through the applicable delete controls or by uninstalling the app. Source and output files controlled by the user remain under the user's control. ONNELLAB retains no app content on its servers.

7. Security and children's privacy

ONNELLAB applies reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to data that must be transmitted. Aligna is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes and contact

If app functionality or legal or store requirements change, this document will be updated and its last-updated date will change.

Privacy questions or deletion requests: onnellab.app@gmail.com
