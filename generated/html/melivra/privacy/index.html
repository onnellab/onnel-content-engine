Melivra Privacy Policy

한국어 · ONNELLAB

This Privacy Policy applies to the Melivra app provided by ONNELLAB.

Last updated: 2026-07-30

1. Accounts and direct identifiers

Melivra does not require registration or sign-in with a name, email address, or phone number. Except for an optional server feature described below, app content and usage history are not transmitted to ONNELLAB.

2. Data accessed or stored on the device

  • Local audio files and their media metadata
  • Library entries, playback history, bookmarks, playlists, search history, AI results, and app preferences stored on the device
  • Purchase entitlement and AI credit balance information
  • Library browsing and ordinary audio playback occur on the device without uploading the audio.
  • AI transcript or translation processing starts only after the user selects audio and approves the disclosed credit estimate and upload.

3. Optional server processing

  • An anonymous server-issued subject identifier
  • The selected audio file for an approved AI job
  • Store product and transaction identifiers used to verify AI credit purchases
  • AI job status, credit ledger entries, transcripts, translations, and generated result metadata

This data is used only to verify purchases, maintain the AI credit balance, process the requested transcript or translation, return the result, prevent duplicate grants, and handle operational support.

4. Payment information

In-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Neither Melivra nor ONNELLAB accesses or stores card or bank-account details. Product, transaction, and entitlement information supplied by the store may be used to verify and restore purchases.

5. Advertising, analytics, and sharing

Melivra does not use advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking tools. Data is not sold or shared outside the purposes described above. If the user deliberately hands content to another app or service through the operating-system share features, that service's policy applies.

6. Retention and deletion

Uploaded source audio is not retained long-term after result generation. Job, result, transaction-verification, and credit-ledger records may be retained for service delivery, duplicate-payment prevention, accounting, dispute handling, and legal obligations. Deletion questions or requests can be sent to the contact address below.

7. Security and children's privacy

ONNELLAB applies reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to data that must be transmitted. Melivra is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes and contact

If app functionality or legal or store requirements change, this document will be updated and its last-updated date will change.

Privacy questions or deletion requests: onnellab.app@gmail.com
