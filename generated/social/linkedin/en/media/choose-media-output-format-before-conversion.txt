The clearest way to choose an output format before converting a media file is to separate the decision from the tool.

Choose the destination first, then check its accepted container, codecs, limits, and the features you must preserve. If existing streams meet those requirements, passthrough or remux may be enough.

Before changing tools:
- Preserve the original. Work on a duplicate or confirm that the tool creates a separate output. Do not use the conversion result as the only archive copy.
- Inspect the source. Record the container, video codec, audio codec, dimensions, frame rate, sample rate, channels, subtitle tracks, duration, metadata, transparency, and file size as relevant.
