A reliable plan for how to organize downloaded files without building a complicated folder system starts with the result and constraints.

Treat Downloads as an inbox, not as a permanent library. Process each completed download into one of four durable destinations: Action for something that needs a next step, Reference for information you expect to reuse, Archive for closed records you must retain, and Temporary for disposable files with a review or deletion date.

Before changing tools:
- Create Action, Reference, Archive, and Temporary in a stable backed-up location.
- Write a simple naming rule and a Temporary review interval.
- Sort Downloads by oldest first and separate incomplete or suspicious items without opening them.
