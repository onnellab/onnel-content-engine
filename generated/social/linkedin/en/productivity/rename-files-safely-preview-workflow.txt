The clearest way to rename batches of files without applying mistakes is to separate the decision from the tool.

Work on a copy when the files matter, define one small rule at a time, and inspect a complete file renaming preview before applying anything. Check that every original maps to one unique destination, preserve extensions unless changing them is intentional, test a representative subset, and verify the resulting files in their destination app.

Before changing tools:
- Define the target pattern. Write one example old name and its exact desired result. Use an unambiguous structure such as `2026-08_client_subject_001.ext`.
