A reliable plan for how to reuse frequently typed text without losing the context that makes it accurate starts with the result and constraints.

Save only the stable wording, mark details that change, and keep a note about when the text applies. Before sending, check the recipient, replace every placeholder and read the complete message.

Before changing tools:
- Choose one recurring task. Start with a few low-risk replies you genuinely repeat. Keep sentences that remain true across those cases.
- Remove case-specific details. Replace dates, names and links with conspicuous markers. Remove promises, prices and exceptions unless you will verify them for each use.
