How do you separate what a source says from what you conclude.

Give every quotation or paraphrase a source and a precise locator. Write your interpretation separately, preserve the source’s limits, and record what needs checking before you strengthen a conclusion.

- Capture the source and locator. Record a title, author or organization when available, and a link or file identifier. Add the page, section, paragraph, or timestamp needed to find the passage. For changing material, record the version or access date. A title alone may not identify the passage you used.
