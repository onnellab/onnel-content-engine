The clearest way to record uncertainty without making a research note vague or falsely confident is to separate the decision from the tool.

Mark what is unknown, disputed or provisional; explain why, link the evidence and write a specific next check. Keep earlier reasoning when updating the note.

- Isolate the claim. Write one checkable statement. Separate an event's date from your explanation of why it happened; they may need different evidence.
- Name the uncertainty and its reason. Write “unknown: no dated record found,” not simply “maybe.” A search that found nothing does not prove that no record exists.
