A reliable plan for how to choose a safe working copy before editing an audio file starts with the result and constraints.

Identify and keep the original, create a separate working copy with a clear name, and confirm that copy is the file you open. Export to a new file and listen to the result before removing anything.

- Keep an identifiable original, make a separate working copy and check the exported audio before deciding what to keep or remove.
