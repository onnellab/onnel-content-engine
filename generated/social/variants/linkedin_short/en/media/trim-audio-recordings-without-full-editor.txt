A reliable plan for how to trim an audio recording without turning it into a full editing project starts with the result and constraints.

Keep the original, work on a copy, set in and out points by listening as well as viewing the waveform, preview both boundaries, and export a new file deliberately. Prefer lossless output when preserving decoded audio matters; re-encode only for a clear compatibility or size need.

- Preserve the original. Back it up or duplicate it, and confirm the working file opens and plays.
- Define the destination. Decide whether the clip is for archive, transcription, presentation, messaging, or another known use.
